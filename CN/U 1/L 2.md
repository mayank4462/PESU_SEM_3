# PART 1 — MUST KNOW

## 1. Network Edge

The **network edge** refers to the hosts/end systems that are connected to the Internet.

### Hosts include:

- Clients
- Servers
- End systems

Examples:

- Smartphones
- Laptops
- PCs
- Servers

### Important point

Servers can be located in **data centers**.

So remember:

```
Network Edge
│
├── Hosts
│   ├── Clients
│   └── Servers
│
└── End Systems
```

# 14. Fiber Optic Cable

Fiber optic cable uses **light pulses** to carry data.

The PDF describes it as:

> Glass fiber carrying light pulses, each pulse a bit.

### Characteristics

- High-speed operation
- Point-to-point
- Transmission rates of **10s–100s Gbps**
- Low error rate
- Repeaters spaced far apart
- Immune to electromagnetic noise

### Most important advantages

1. Very high speed
2. Low error rate
3. Long distance between repeaters
4. Immune to electromagnetic noise

---

# 15. Radio

Wireless radio uses signals carried in the **electromagnetic spectrum**.

### Characteristics

- No physical "wire"
- Broadcast
- Half-duplex
- Propagation environment affects performance

### Propagation effects

The PDF mentions:

- Reflection
- Obstruction by objects
- Interference

These can affect wireless communication.

### 1. Terrestrial microwave

### 2. Wireless LAN — Wi-Fi

### 3. Wide-area cellular

- 4G cellular: **10s Mbps**

### 4. Satellite

The slide gives:

---

# 17. Half-Duplex

The PDF specifically explains half-duplex:

> Data can only be transmitted in one direction at a time.

Meaning:

```
A ─────→ B
```

Then:

```
A ←───── B
```

But both directions cannot transmit simultaneously.

### Full-duplex

Both directions can transmit simultaneously.

```
A ⇄ B
```

### Remember:

**Half-duplex = one direction at a time**

**Full-duplex = both directions simultaneously**

# PART 5 — IMPORTANT COMPARISONS

## 1. Guided vs Unguided Media

|Guided|Unguided|
|---|---|
|Signal travels through solid medium|Signal propagates freely|
|Physical medium is used|No physical wire required|
|Twisted pair|Radio|
|Coaxial cable|—|
|Fiber optic|—|

# 2. WLAN vs Cellular

|WLAN|Cellular|
|---|---|
|Local area|Wide area|
|Wi-Fi|4G/5G|
|802.11 b/g/n|Mobile/cellular network|
|11, 54, 450 Mbps shown|10s Mbps shown|
|Typically within/around building|Wide geographical coverage|

---

# 3. Twisted Pair vs Coaxial vs Fiber

|Feature|Twisted Pair|Coaxial|Fiber|
|---|---|---|---|
|Medium|Copper|Copper|Glass fiber|
|Structure|Two insulated copper wires|Two concentric copper conductors|Glass fiber|
|Guided?|Yes|Yes|Yes|
|Key property|Simple copper medium|Broadband, bidirectional|Very high speed|
|Speed shown|Cat 5: 100 Mbps; Cat 6: 1 Gbps|100s Mbps/channel|10s–100s Gbps|

---

# 4. Half-Duplex vs Full-Duplex

|Half-Duplex|Full-Duplex|
|---|---|
|One direction at a time|Both directions simultaneously|
|A → B, then B → A|A ⇄ B|
|Cannot transmit both ways at the same time|Can transmit both ways at the same time|

The PDF explicitly defines **half-duplex**; the full-duplex contrast is useful for understanding the term.

> Physical media are the media through which signals propagate between network devices. They are classified into **guided and unguided media**.

### Guided media

Signals propagate through a solid medium.

**1. Twisted Pair**

- Two insulated copper wires
- Category 5 → 100 Mbps
- Category 6 → 1 Gbps

**2. Coaxial Cable**

- Two concentric copper conductors
- Bidirectional
- Broadband
- Multiple frequency channels

**3. Fiber Optic Cable**

- Glass fiber carrying light pulses
- 10s–100s Gbps
- Low error rate
- Immune to electromagnetic noise

### Unguided media

Signals propagate freely.

**Radio**

- Uses electromagnetic spectrum
- No physical wire
- Affected by reflection, obstruction and interference

# PART 11 — ULTRA-IMPORTANT EXAM TRAPS

### Trap 1

**Access network ≠ Internet core**

Access network connects:

**End system → First/edge router**

---

### Trap 2

**Host ≠ Router**

Host/end system is at the network edge.

Router forwards network traffic.

---

### Trap 3

**Fiber ≠ Radio**

Fiber is **guided**.

Radio is **unguided**.

---

### Trap 4

**Twisted pair ≠ coaxial**

Twisted pair:

> Two insulated copper wires.

Coaxial:

> Two concentric copper conductors.

---

### Trap 5

**WLAN ≠ cellular**

WLAN:

> Local area + Wi-Fi

Cellular:

> Wide area + 4G/5G

---

### Trap 6

**Half-duplex does NOT mean one-way communication**

It means:

> Both directions are possible, but **only one direction at a time**.

---

### Trap 7

**Fiber's important advantage**

Don't just write "fast."

The PDF specifically highlights:

- High speed
- Low error rate
- Repeaters far apart
- Immunity to electromagnetic noise
